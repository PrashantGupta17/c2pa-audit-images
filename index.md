# C2PA Durability Audit

This repository hosts research artifacts for an academic study measuring the durability of C2PA (Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity) credentials across social media platforms.

## About the project

The C2PA standard provides cryptographic provenance metadata for digital media. This study measures, empirically, whether platforms preserve these credentials when content is uploaded — across Mastodon, Bluesky, Facebook, Instagram, WordPress, and (where feasible) other platforms. Findings are intended for academic publication at a security venue.

## Scope

- Read-only / measurement use of platform APIs
- Single-researcher project, non-commercial, academic in purpose
- Test posts are uploaded to dedicated research accounts owned by the researcher
- Source code: not published publicly during the study
