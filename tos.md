# Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026-06-29

This page describes the terms under which the C2PA Durability Audit research project (the "Project") uses platform APIs and services.

## Purpose

The Project is an academic research effort measuring the durability of C2PA content credentials across social media platforms. It is non-commercial and conducted by a single researcher.

## API usage

- The Project accesses platform APIs solely for the purpose of measuring whether C2PA credentials are preserved or stripped during platform upload/processing.
- All test content is generated by the researcher specifically for this study. No third-party content is uploaded.
- All test posts are made to research-dedicated accounts owned by the researcher.
- Where a platform offers sandbox or testing modes, the Project uses those rather than production endpoints whenever sufficient for the measurement.

## No commercial use

The Project, its data, and its findings will not be used commercially. The findings will be published academically at a peer-reviewed security venue.

## No warranty

This Project is provided as-is for research purposes. No warranty is offered as to the accuracy or completeness of any findings prior to peer review.

## Changes

The researcher may update these terms as the study evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
